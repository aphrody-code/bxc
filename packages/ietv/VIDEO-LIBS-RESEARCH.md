## 🔍 Criteria de sélection

1. **Video Player**
   - Bun-compatible ou pure TS/JS
   - Support formats: MP4, WebM, HLS, DASH
   - Responsive + mobile-ready
   - Streaming optimisé
   - Lite footprint

2. **Video Compression**
   - Modernes (H.265, AV1, VP9)
   - Quality/size tradeoff
   - Batch processing
   - Command-line + SDK
   - Actif + maintained

3. **Video Search**
   - Metadata indexing
   - Fuzzy matching
   - Multi-language
   - Fast queries
   - Cross-platform
